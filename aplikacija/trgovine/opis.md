## Puni opis (hr)

Freshy's je kuharica za pse. Upišeš koliko tvoj pas teži i koliko se kreće, a svaki recept pokaže koliko grama čega treba baš njemu.

U aplikaciji:
- 330 recepata za pse, s količinama u gramima;
- vaga dnevne potrebe po smjernicama AAHA (Američko udruženje bolnica za male životinje);
- način kuhanja korak po korak, s minutnicom koja javi i kad je mobitel zaključan;
- plan za tjedan i popis za kupnju s već zbrojenim količinama;
- upozorenja uz sastojke koje pas ne smije jesti (npr. ksilitol u maslacu od kikirikija);
- Moji ljubimci: profil psa, vaganja i koliko hrane mu dnevno treba.

Bez računa sve ostaje samo na tvom mobitelu. S računom (e-mail i lozinka) ljubimci su ti na svakom uređaju.

Freshy's ne zamjenjuje veterinara. Ne postavlja dijagnozu i ne savjetuje liječenje. Prije veće promjene prehrane, kod šteneta,
trudne kuje ili psa s bolešću, pitaj veterinara.

Freshy's je iz Osijeka.

## Full description (en)

Freshy's is a cookbook for dogs. Enter how much your dog weighs and how active it is, and every recipe shows how many grams of each
ingredient your dog needs.

In the app:
- 330 dog recipes, with amounts in grams;
- a daily needs calculator based on the AAHA guidelines (American Animal Hospital Association);
- step-by-step cooking mode, with a timer that rings even when the phone is locked;
- a plan for the week and a shopping list with the amounts already added up;
- warnings next to ingredients dogs must not eat (for example xylitol in peanut butter);
- My pets: your dog's profile, weigh-ins and how much food it needs a day.

Without an account everything stays on your phone. With an account (e-mail and password) your pets are on every device.

Freshy's doesn't replace a vet. It doesn't diagnose and doesn't advise on treatment. Before a big change in diet, and for puppies,
pregnant dogs or dogs with an illness, ask your vet.

Freshy's is made in Osijek, Croatia.
